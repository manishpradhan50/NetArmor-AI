KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

🔍
How does NetArmor AI detect zero-day phishing websites? ▼
NetArmor AI employs a multi-vector heuristic and ML feature extractor. It inspects structural anomalies such as embedded IP addresses, suspicious '@' symbols, deep multi-level subdomains, typo-squatted brand names, and insecure protocols to accurately predict phishing threats even before traditional blocklists list them.
What steps should I take immediately after a financial cyber fraud? ▼
Act within the "Golden Hour" (first 2 hours): 1. Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930 immediately to freeze recipient accounts. 2. Lodge a formal report at cybercrime.gov.in. 3. Contact your bank's emergency desk to block debit/credit cards and net banking access. 4. Preserve digital evidence: transaction UTR numbers, SMS messages, call logs, and screenshots.
How does the Cyber Complaint tracking system work? ▼
When you submit a complaint in NetArmor AI, the system automatically assigns a unique tracking ID (e.g. NET-CMP-2026-XXXX) and stores the incident securely in Cloud Firestore. You can inspect its status at any time across 5 milestones: Submitted, Under Review, In Progress, Resolved, and Closed.
Can I upload evidence files (screenshots, PDFs) with my complaint? ▼
Yes! The NetArmor AI complaint portal supports file attachments up to 10MB (PNG, JPG, PDF, TXT, DOCX). The files are securely processed and linked to your incident report.
How does the Email NLP Analyzer identify phishing emails? ▼
The Email Analyzer utilizes TF-IDF Vectorization and an XGBoost machine learning classifier trained on cybersecurity datasets. It evaluates psychological urgency lures, artificial deadlines, fake credential-verification demands, and fraudulent phrasing.
